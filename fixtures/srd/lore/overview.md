# 🌌 The Structure of the Multiverse (The Great Wheel)

The D&D universe is arranged in a cosmic design known as The Great Wheel, separating planes of existence by alignment, elements, and raw magical energy.

## 1. The Material Plane & Echoes

- The Material Plane: The physical world where standard mortal campaigns take place (e.g., Toril, Oerth, Krynn).
- The Feywild: A vibrant, unfiltered reflection of the Material Plane overflowing with wild, untamed magic, emotion, and the archfey.
- The Shadowfell: A bleak, monochrome mirror world of despair, decay, and shifting shadows ruled by entities like the Raven Queen.

## 2. The Transitive Planes

- The Astral Plane: The plane of thought, silver cords, and starlight. It is an endless silvery void used for travel between outer realms, littered with the petrified bodies of dead gods.
- The Ethereal Plane: A misty, ghostly dimension coexistent with the Material Plane, used by spirits and phase-shifting creatures.

## 3. The Inner Planes (The Elemental Chaos)

The foundational building blocks of physical matter, consisting of the four major Elemental Planes surrounding the roiling Elemental Chaos:
- 🔥 Plane of Fire | ⛰️ Plane of Earth | 🌪️ Plane of Air | 🌊 Plane of Water

> [!secret] ## 4. The Outer Planes (The Ideological Realms)
> 
> 
> > [!secret] Sixteen distinct planes corresponding to the traditional moral alignments, serving as the domains of gods and the final resting places for mortal souls:
> > [!secret] - Mount Celestia: The ultimate realm of Lawful Good, structured as a radiant seven-tiered mountain.
> > [!secret] - The Nine Hells (Baator): A strictly bureaucratic nightmare of Lawful Evil, divided into nine layers governed by Asmodeus and his Archdevils.
> > [!secret] - The Abyss: An infinite, chaotic expanse of Pure Evil, producing ravenous demons led by Demon Lords like Demogorgon and Orcus.
> > [!secret] - Mechanus: The clockwork plane of Absolute Neutrality and supreme order, populated by the Modrons.

# ⏳ The Eras of History (The Forgotten Realms Standard)

1. The Days of Thunder: Ancient pre-history ruled by the Creator Races (Batrachi, Aearee, Sarrukh). Dragons and Giants overthrow them, sparking a millennia-long war for dominance.
2. The First Flowering: Elves establish massive empires and cast the First Sundering, separating the continent of Faerûn from the island of Evermeet.
3. The Age of Netheril: Human spellcasters discover ancient magical scrolls and build floating cities, utilizing world-bending spells until the god of magic is killed, causing the cataclysmic collapse of high magic.
4. The Era of Upheaval (Recent History): Marked by catastrophic world events engineered by gods and magical anomalies:
	• The Time of Troubles: Gods are stripped of divinity and forced to walk the earth as mortals.
	• The Spellplague: Mystra is assassinated, causing a raw blue magical fire to rewrite the geography of the planes.
	• The Second Sundering: The universe fixes itself, stabilizing the cosmic boundaries for current adventures.

> [!secret]- # 🛠️ DM Cheat Sheet: Core Constants of D&D Lore
>
> - The Blood War: An eternal, cosmic war of attrition fought between the Devils (Baator) and Demons (The Abyss) over the control of mortal souls. If either side wins, the material plane falls.
> - The Weave: Raw magical energy is lethal; The Weave is a protective fabric maintained by the goddess of magic that allows mortals to safely cast spells.
> - The Dawn War: The mythological war where the Gods (creators of order) banded together to defeat the Primals/Titans (avatars of elemental chaos) to secure the current cosmos.
