# Map
*Adventuring gear*  

- **Cost**: 1 gp

If you consult an accurate Map, you gain a +5 bonus to Wisdom ([Survival](rules/skills.md#Survival)) checks you make to find your way in the place represented on it.

*Source: the Free Rules (2024)*
