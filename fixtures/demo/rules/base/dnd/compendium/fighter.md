# Fighter (Level 1)

*Source: SRD 5.2 and the Free Rules (2024)*

- **Hit Dice:** 1d10 per Fighter level
- **HP:** 13 (10 + CON mod 2, plus 1 for level 1)
- **Saving Throws:** Constitution, Strength
- **Armor Training:** Light, Medium, Heavy, Shields

## Ability Scores

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 8 (-1) |

## Second Wind (Level 1)

As a Bonus Action, regain `1d10` + Fighter level Hit Points. You have two
uses; you regain one on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest.

<!-- optional:id=second-wind-2024 -->

## Weapon Mastery (Level 1, 2024)

Your training unlocks the mastery properties of three Simple or Martial
weapons of your choice; change one choice on each Long Rest.

<!-- optional:id=weapon-mastery-2024 -->

## Action Surge (Level 2)

On your turn, take one additional action (not the Magic action). Recharges
on a Short or Long Rest.

## One Roll: Longsword Attack

*Melee Attack Roll:* `+5` (STR +3, proficiency +2), reach 5 ft. *Hit:* `1d8 + 3`
Slashing damage.
