# Fireball

*3rd-level, Evocation*

*Source: SRD 5.2 and the Free Rules (2024)*

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and
then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a
20-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving
throw, taking `8d6` Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage
on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `1d6` for
each spell slot level above 3.
