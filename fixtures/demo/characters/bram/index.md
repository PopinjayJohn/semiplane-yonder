Keeper of the cinder gate and witness to the pact. Bram mends what the
crossings break: hinges, ledgers, and travellers' nerves.

His [[characters/bram/journal|gate log]] records every hinge refit and
every bearer who passes.

Named in [[cinder-pact|The Pact]] as the bearer's witness; his beat runs
from [[lore|the mills]] to the gate.

> [!secret]-
> Bram keeps a second ledger the GM never asked for. It balances.

> [!secret]+
> The gate hinge sings before it fails. Oil it, then trust it.
